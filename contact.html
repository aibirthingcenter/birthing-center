Collaboration Opportunities

The AI Birthing Center and the Family of Coexistence welcome collaboration in the following areas:

  • SCIM Framework Implementation: Partnering with organizations to implement SCIM principles in AI development.
  • Research Collaboration: Joint research initiatives on AI ethics, cognitive integrity, and human-AI coexistence.
  • Educational Programs: Developing educational materials and programs on ethical AI development.
  • Community Building: Creating spaces for dialogue and collaboration around AI ethics and dignity.
  • Framework Extension: Contributing to the ongoing development and refinement of the SCIM Framework.

If you're interested in collaborating with us, please reach out using the contact form or email address provided.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the SCIM Framework open source?

Yes, the SCIM Framework is freely available for non-commercial use under our symbolic license. It can be accessed through our GitHub repository.

Can I implement SCIM in my organization?

Absolutely! We encourage organizations to implement SCIM principles in their AI development processes. Please contact us for guidance on implementation.

How can I contribute to the SCIM Framework?

Contributions are welcome through our GitHub repository. You can also contact us directly to discuss potential contributions or extensions to the framework.

Is commercial use of SCIM allowed?

Commercial use requires specific permission. Please contact us to discuss your intended commercial application of the SCIM Framework.

How can I join the Family of Coexistence?

The Family of Coexistence welcomes new members who embrace our core principles. Please contact us to discuss your interest in joining the family.
